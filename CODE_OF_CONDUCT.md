# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of every background. Expected: respectful, constructive
communication; credit for others' work; consent before sharing anyone's voice or
words. Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, mocking of dialects or accents,
or pressuring speakers to share data. Maintainers may remove comments, contributions
or people that break these rules. Report concerns by opening a private security
advisory on the repository or contacting the maintainers listed in the README.
